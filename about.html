About NUS Tour

Introduction

Welcome to NUS TOUR! Here is where you get an opportunity to explore around the top university in Asia -- NUS! Join now to experience a fully dedicated school tour just for you!

How to Book

  1. Simple go to Book Now, choose the date and time you wish to make a visit!
  2. Take note that a minimum of 5 Adult or equivalent children is required to start the tour (1 Adult = 2 Children), with AT LEAST 1 Adult required to be present.
  3. There can be a maximum of only 20 person (No matter Adult or Children) per tour.
  4. After confirming your slot, enter your email and you will be directed to pay the confirmation fee of $5 per person. Please Pay the amount and upload the proof of payment.
  5. You will receive an email once the booking is confirmed. Futher details will be given through the email.
